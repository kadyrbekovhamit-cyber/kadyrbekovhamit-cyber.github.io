A few useful things to know

Clear from
the start.

Will I need to manage another dashboard?+

TaskAlly is a managed service. The tools work in the background, with updates and handovers shaped around how you run your business.

Can we start with just one task?+

That is the proposed approach: agree one useful workflow, check the setup, and review it before deciding what to add.

What happens when a person is needed?+

Human handover points should be agreed during setup. Unclear questions, exceptions and decisions needing judgement should have a defined route to your team.

Will it work with the tools I already use?+

The existing tools and their integration options need to be checked first. Compatibility and scope are agreed before a workflow is put in place.
