Interpretation limits

  • This is an offline analytical specimen, not an audit opinion or a finding of misconduct.
  • Equal payment attributes do not establish that two cash transfers occurred.
  • Awards and obligations are excluded from cash totals; currencies are never converted or combined.
  • Passing these specified checks does not prove the population is accurate, complete or free of irregularities.
  • Control totals, date meanings, entry conventions and source authenticity require independent validation.
